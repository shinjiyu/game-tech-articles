## 摘要摘录

Ray tracing (RT) generates photorealistic images by simulating the physical behavior of light as it interacts with surfaces in a virtual scene. In film production and high-end rendering pipelines, this technique computes global illumination, reflections, refractions, and soft shadows by tracing millions of light rays per frame and accurately modeling their bounces and attenuation. Contemporary GPUs incorporate specialized ray tracing hardware to accelerate key operations in the traversal pipeline.
