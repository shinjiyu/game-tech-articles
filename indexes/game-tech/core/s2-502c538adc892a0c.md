## 摘要摘录

In this paper, we present a novel neural global illumination approach that enables multi-frequency reflections in dynamic scenes. Our method utilizes object-centric, spatial feature grids as the core framework to model rendering effects implicitly. A lightweight scene query, based on single-bounce ray tracing, is then performed on these feature grids to extract principal and secondary features separately. The principal features can capture a wide range of relatively low-frequency global illumination effects, such as diffuse indirect lighting and reflections on rough surfaces.
