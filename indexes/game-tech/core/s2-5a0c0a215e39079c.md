## 摘要摘录

A considerable amount of research has been devoted to the development of realistic lighting in computer graphics, aiming to render a scene that closely resembles realworld lighting. Global illumination techniques aim to solve the rendering equation to simulate realistic lighting by taking into account direct and indirect lighting. Even though indirect illumination is required for realism in computer graphics, its accurate calculation can be very computationally expensive. The aim of this study is to build a real-time 2D Global Illumination engine with accurate lighting and soft shadows which supports a completely dynamic scene encompassing a multitude of lights and occluders efficiently.
